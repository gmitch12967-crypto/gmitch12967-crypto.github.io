How do I open my Offline Edition .zip file?

Windows: Right-click the .zip file, click Extract All..., open the new folder, and double-click the .html file inside. It opens right in Chrome or Edge with no internet needed.
Mac: Double-click the .zip file to unzip it, then double-click the .html file to open it in Safari or Chrome.

Why does it open in my web browser if it works offline?

Your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox) can open local .html files straight from your computer's hard drive or USB stick with Wi-Fi completely turned off. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent online.

My printed Passport / Visa photo came out slightly too big or too small

When printing the 4×6 inch or A4 sheet, make sure your printer dialog is set to Actual Size (100% scale) — not "Fit to Page" or "Shrink to Fit." If you print at CVS or Walgreens on 4×6 photo paper, pick the 4×6 sheet option and uncheck auto-crop if shown.

A website still says my photo is too big after shrinking

Pick a smaller limit under 2. Maximum File Size Needed (for example, switch from Under 2 MB to Under 500 KB or Under 200 KB) and choose JPG format, then save again.

Does Photo Privacy Cleaner really remove my GPS home location?

Yes. It redraws your image pixels into a brand-new JPEG inside your browser, which strips out all hidden EXIF tags, GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers, and timestamps.

Excel opened my CSV with everything crammed into Column A

Open Tool #4 (International CSV & Date Fixer). If you are in Europe or Latin America, set Output Separator to Semicolon (;). If you are in the US or UK, set it to Comma (,). Then save and double-click the new file in Excel.

Only 100 lines show on screen in Tool #1 — did it lose my other lines?

No! To keep your browser fast on big spreadsheets, only the first 100 lines are previewed on screen. When you click Save Clean File, every clean line in your file is saved (up to 25 lines in the free online trial, or unlimited lines in the Offline Edition).

Will my QR code ever expire or ask for a monthly fee later?

Never. Your real website URL or Wi-Fi password is encoded directly into the QR squares with zero redirect servers. Even if our website disappeared years from now, your printed QR codes will still scan forever.
