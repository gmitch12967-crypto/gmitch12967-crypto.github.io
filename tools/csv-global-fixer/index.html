How to Use & Helpful Tips

  • Why Excel scrambles CSVs: US/UK Excel expects commas (,), while many European and Latin American versions expect semicolons (;).
  • How to fix it: Pick your file, choose the separator your Excel uses, pick your preferred date format, and click 3. Save Fixed File.
  • Universal tip: Choosing YYYY-MM-DD prevents Excel from ever mixing up the day and the month.
