We guide you step-by-step through Samsung One UI so you cannot make a mistake. Nothing is hidden or confusing.
1
Reset Advertising ID (Sever Cross-App Tracking)
Action Needed
Why: Ad networks connect your activity in old uninstalled apps to new apps through this ID. Resetting it resets your advertising fingerprint to zero.
📲 Exact Samsung S10+ Quick Path:
Open phone Settings ⚙️ → tap Google → tap Ads → tap Reset advertising ID → Confirm.
2
Wipe "Not Installed" Play Store Cloud Records
Action Needed
Why: Google Play logs every app you ever downloaded to your Google account. You can purge this cloud log directly.
📲 Exact Google Play Steps:
1. Open Play Store → tap your Profile Photo (top-right).
2. Tap Manage apps & device → tap Manage tab.
3. Change filter from "Installed" to "Not installed" → select old apps and tap the Trash Can 🗑️.
3
Lock Down Background Location & Mic Permissions
Action Needed
Why: Prevent idle apps from accessing your GPS location, camera, or microphone while in your pocket.
📲 Exact Samsung S10+ Quick Path:
Open phone Settings ⚙️ → tap Privacy → tap Permission manager → tap Location → set apps to "Only while using the app".
