🔒 Galaxy S10+ Guided Privacy Walkthrough
We guide you step-by-step through Samsung One UI so you cannot make a mistake. Nothing is hidden or confusing.
1
Reset Android Advertising ID (GAID)
Recommended
Why: Companies use your Advertising ID to link what you do in old uninstalled apps to new apps you download. Resetting it severs that tracking chain completely.

How: Tap the blue button below to open your phone's Ads screen. Then tap "Reset advertising ID" and tap "Confirm".
🚀 Open Ads Settings
2
Remove "Not Installed" Play Library Records
Recommended
Why: Google Play keeps a cloud log of every app you ever downloaded. You can remove uninstalled apps from your visible library.

How: Tap Open Play Store → tap your Profile icon (top right) → Manage apps & device → tap Manage tab → switch filter to "Not installed" → select apps and tap the Trash icon.
📱 Open Google Play
3
Audit Location & Camera Permissions
Optional
Why: Prevent idle apps from accessing your GPS location or microphone in the background.

How: Tap Open Privacy Settings → tap Permission Manager → review which apps have "Allowed all the time" and change them to "Only while using the app".
🛡️ Open Privacy Settings
